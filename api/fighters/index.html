    Fighters¶

    The two fighters: player (you) and opponent. Move them, turn them, squash them, tint them, pose them, re-skin them, pin them to the screen like sprites, outline them. All of it only in your own picture, so go wild.

    How to use it

    Every property here has several names for the same two fighters:

    Name Who
    player / puppet[0] you
    opponent / puppet[1] the other one
    boyfriend / dad by the chart's sides: whoever plays the Boyfriend part / the opponent part - the same as player / opponent unless you play the opponent side

    Where: in a fight, the fighters in the world - seen only by you, the real players don't move. In practice, the two puppets beside the lanes follow most of it (position, size, squash, turn, tint, poses, body parts, pinning); clip*, outline*, depth and facing are fights only.

    Put one back with resetPuppet(who[, duration]) or reset(player, duration). Shortcuts: pose, setPose, setSkin, setModel, swapFighters, bind, focus. Body parts: Body parts. The whole picture: The fighters.

    Psych: boyfriend.x, dad.alpha, characterPlayAnim, setObjectCamera · Kade: boyfriend, dad

    Example code¶

    -- The opponent slides in from the right into the middle, looks at you, then goes back.
function onEvent(p)
    opponent.pinned = true
    opponent.screenX = 1.3
    opponent.screenY = opponent.homeY
    opponent.facing = "camera"
    tween(opponent.screenX, 0.5, beats(2), "cubicInOut")
    after(beats(8), function()
        tween(opponent.screenX, opponent.homeX, beats(2), "cubicInOut")
        after(beats(2), function() resetPuppet("opponent") end)
    end)
end

    colour player.color · opponent.color · boyfriend.color · dad.color · puppet[i].color = #FFFFFF

    Multiplied onto the skin - and onto its armor, cape and head item.

    player.visible¶

    switch player.visible · opponent.visible · boyfriend.visible · dad.visible · puppet[i].visible = true

    false = gone.

    player.animate¶

    switch player.animate · opponent.animate · boyfriend.animate · dad.animate · puppet[i].animate = true

    false = MineFunkin's own animation stops: it stands in its rest pose and only your values (and the body parts') move it. Wins over pose.

    player.squash¶

    number player.squash · opponent.squash · boyfriend.squash · dad.squash · puppet[i].squash = 0

    Squash and stretch, feet on the ground, volume kept: 0.3 = shorter and wider, -0.3 = taller and thinner.

    player.pose¶

    choice player.pose · opponent.pose · boyfriend.pose · dad.pose · puppet[i].pose = "none"

    Plays one of MineFunkin's own poses: a lane's pose pops and holds like a held note, "death" sits it down, "none" lets go (a held pose eases out like a released note). While it's set, the notes don't change it. Your own poses: setPose.

    One of: "none", "left", "down", "up", "right", "death" (what they do).

    player.skin¶

    text player.skin · opponent.skin · boyfriend.skin · dad.skin · puppet[i].skin = ""

    Its Minecraft skin: "" = its own, "player" / "opponent" = that fighter's own (so the two can swap), or a 64x64 skin .png from the song folder (or its events/ / skins/ folder) - at most 8 files, 1 MB each.

    player.model¶

    choice player.model · opponent.model · boyfriend.model · dad.model · puppet[i].model = "default"

    The arm shape: "default" (what the skin says), "wide" (Steve, 4 pixels) or "slim" (Alex, 3 pixels).

    One of: "default", "wide", "slim".

    player.pinned¶

    switch player.pinned · opponent.pinned · boyfriend.pinned · dad.pinned · puppet[i].pinned = false

    true = stuck to a spot on the screen like a sprite: screenX / screenY place its feet, whatever the camera does. false = back where the fight wants it, at once.

    player.place¶

    choice player.place · opponent.place · boyfriend.place · dad.place · puppet[i].place = ""

    Which spot of the fight it stands on: "bf" (the spot in front, where the camera stands behind), "dad" (the far spot), "" = the fight's own (you in front). Set it with bind(who, "bf" | "dad") - that sets both fighters, so they swap. The camera always stays behind the "bf" spot.

    One of: "", "bf", "dad".

    player.screenX¶

    number player.screenX · opponent.screenX · boyfriend.screenX · dad.screenX · puppet[i].screenX = 0.5

    share of the screen

    A pinned fighter's feet, from left to right: 0 = the left edge, 1 = the right edge. Beyond = off the screen.

    player.screenY¶

    number player.screenY · opponent.screenY · boyfriend.screenY · dad.screenY · puppet[i].screenY = 0.9

    share of the screen

    A pinned fighter's feet, from top to bottom: 0.9 = near the bottom. Above 1 = the feet below the bottom edge.

    player.depth¶

    number player.depth · opponent.depth · boyfriend.depth · dad.depth · puppet[i].depth = 0

    blocks

    How far from the camera a pinned fighter stands, in blocks: near = big. 0 = as far as it really is. The camera's zoom still makes it bigger or smaller.

    player.screenHeight¶

    number player.screenHeight · opponent.screenHeight · boyfriend.screenHeight · dad.screenHeight · puppet[i].screenHeight = 0

    share of the screen

    A pinned fighter's height as a share of the screen (1 = as tall as the screen), whatever the camera's lens. 0 = depth decides.

    player.clipLeft¶

    number player.clipLeft · opponent.clipLeft · boyfriend.clipLeft · dad.clipLeft · puppet[i].clipLeft = 0

    share of the screenfights only

    Cuts the fighter off left of this share of the screen (0 = not cut). With the other three it shows the fighter only inside a box - in a window frame, a TV, a rift.

    -- Only inside the frame drawn at 0.3-0.7 x 0.2-0.8.
player.clipLeft = 0.3
player.clipRight = 0.7
player.clipTop = 0.2
player.clipBottom = 0.8

    player.clipBottom¶

    number player.clipBottom · opponent.clipBottom · boyfriend.clipBottom · dad.clipBottom · puppet[i].clipBottom = 1

    share of the screenfights only

    player.facing¶

    choice player.facing · opponent.facing · boyfriend.facing · dad.facing · puppet[i].facing = "fight"

    fights only

    "fight" = it faces where the fight turns it, "camera" = it looks straight at you (turn still turns it on top).

    One of: "fight", "camera".
