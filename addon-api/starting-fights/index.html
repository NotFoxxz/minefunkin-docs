    Starting fights¶

    Your mod can start a MineFunkin fight: a player against the dummy (your training dummy), against a mob (your boss - see Boss opponents) or two players against each other - anywhere, not only at a Speaker. You build the fight like a kit and send it off; what you leave out gets the same default as a Speaker fight. Needs the right fights.start (see Rights).

    FightRequest request = FightRequest.builder(player, Opponent.dummy(), "bopeebo")
        .difficulty("Hard")
        .onResult(result -> LOGGER.info("{} - {}", result.first().outcome(), result.first().score()))
        .build();

api.fights().start(request);

    That's a whole fight: the player stays where they stand, the dummy appears in front of them, the countdown runs, and when it's over your onResult gets the result.

    The kit¶

    FightRequest.builder(player, opponent, songId) - the three things every fight needs:
    player the first fighter (a ServerPlayer)
    opponent Opponent.dummy(), Opponent.entity(mob) (Boss opponents) or Opponent.player(otherPlayer)
    songId a server song (its folder name, see api.songs().serverSongIds())

    Then, all optional:

    Method Default
    .difficulty("Hard") like the song select: Hard, else Normal, else the first the difficulty by its name
    .character("Pico") the first for a song with characters
    .side(ChartSide.OPPONENT) ChartSide.PLAYER which part of the chart the first player plays (the opponent plays the other)
    .place(...) FightPlace.inPlace() where the fight takes place - below
    .rules(...) FightRules.DEFAULT health, pausing, giving up - below
    .modifiers(Modifier.DOUBLE_TIME, ...) none the modifiers both fighters play with - below
    .ask(true) false ask the players first - below
    .board(...) Board.none() which leaderboard the results go on - below
    .returnTo(posA, posB) back where they stood where the players go after the fight (needs players.teleport)
    .hideEntity(false) true against a mob: leave the real mob alone - Boss opponents
    .bossBar(name, colour) none against a mob: a boss bar for the players around - Boss opponents
    .onResult(result -> ...) none your result, exactly once

    The answer: started or refused¶

    start gives back a FightStart. Most starts are decided within a moment; one that asks the players waits for their answer. Say what should happen in both cases:

    api.fights().start(request)
        .onStarted(fight -> player.sendSystemMessage(Component.literal("Fight!")))
        .onRefused(no -> player.sendSystemMessage(Component.literal("Not now: " + no.reason())));

    Both run on the server thread - right away when it's decided already. start.state() is PENDING, STARTED or REFUSED; start.fight() and start.refusal() hold the outcome. start.withdraw() takes back a start that still waits for an answer.

    A start is refused at once, with the reason - nothing is interrupted, nothing waits in a queue. Try again later if you like:

    Refusal.Reason
    FIGHTING the player is in a fight already
    AT_SPEAKER the player hosts or waits at a Speaker
    AT_RETRO_TV the player is at a Retro TV, in practice or in the chart editor
    WATCHING the player watches a fight from inside
    ASKED_ELSEWHERE the player was asked about another fight and hasn't answered
    NO_MINEFUNKIN the player's game has no MineFunkin
    PLAYER_OFFLINE, NOT_AVAILABLE the player left, is dead or a spectator
    OTHER_WORLD the two players (or the player and the mob) are in different worlds
    ENTITY_GONE the opponent mob is gone (died, removed, unloaded) before the fight could start
    NO_ROOM no spot for the fight - below
    UNKNOWN_SONG, UNKNOWN_CHART, SONGS_NOT_READY no such song / difficulty, or the server is still reading its songs
    NOT_PLAYABLE_VS_PLAYER a side of the song has no notes - against the dummy only
    SPEAKER_BUSY no free Speaker at that place
    DECLINED, NO_ANSWER a player said no, or didn't answer in 30 seconds
    VETOED an allowFight rule said no (message() has its reason)
    WITHDRAWN, SERVER_STOPPING you took it back, or the server stops

    allowFight rules of every mod are asked for your fights too - also against the dummy (then proposal.second() is null).

    Where¶

    Every place follows the arena rules of a Speaker fight: flat ground at the same height, a clear view between the fighters, room for the cameras.

    .place(FightPlace.inPlace())   // the default

    The players stay where they are and are turned toward each other. Against the dummy, the dummy appears in front of the player. Against a mob, its figure stands where the mob stands when that spot fits, else like the dummy. Against a player, both stay when they stand a few blocks apart with a clear view; else the second one is put in front of the first. No room there? MineFunkin looks for a fitting spot up to about 16 blocks away and moves both there - no right needed, they come back afterwards. Nothing found: refused with NO_ROOM.

    .place(FightPlace.at(new Vec3(100.5, 64, 200.5), new Vec3(100.5, 64, 208.5)))

    Two spots (feet) in the first player's world - your boss arena. The second is the opponent's (the dummy's). Needs players.teleport. Spots that break the arena rules: refused with NO_ROOM.

    .place(FightPlace.speaker(speakerPos))

    On the two pads of the Speaker block there, like a Speaker fight (the player side on the right pad). The Speaker must be free; nobody can open it while your fight runs, and players can right-click it to watch.

    After the fight both players get their place and their look back - unless you give your own with .returnTo(firstPos, secondPos) (needs players.teleport; null for one of them = back where they stood).

    Rules¶

    .rules(FightRules.DEFAULT.withPause(false).withForfeit(false))
    Default
    health on health and losing: missing too much makes a fighter lose. Off = no hearts, nobody runs out (like practice)
    pause on the fighters may pause (against a player: one asks, the other accepts)
    forfeit on the fighters may give up once the song runs

    Modifiers¶

    .modifiers(Modifier.DOUBLE_TIME, Modifier.ONE_SHOT)

    Both fighters play with exactly these. The players' own picks in the song select never apply to your fight - leave .modifiers out and it's played without any. EASY beats HARD and ONE_SHOT beats SICK_ONLY when you name both, DOUBLE_TIME beats SLOW, FADE beats FAKEOUT. AUTOPLAY and ADAPTIVE_SPEED are left out - a fight always counts. SHUFFLE takes a seed the server makes, the same for both fighters and everyone watching. The points carry the modifiers' factor like everywhere (Modifier); fight.modifiers() and every result's modifiers() say what was played.

    Asking the players first¶

    .ask(true)

    Every player in the fight gets a question: your mod's name, the opponent, the song - Fight or Not now, 30 seconds to answer. The start waits (PENDING) until all said yes. A "Not now" or no answer refuses it (DECLINED, NO_ANSWER). The server's admin can make every addon fight ask (addonFightsAlwaysAsk) - whatever your mod says.

    Leaderboards¶

    .board(Board.own("finale"))
    Needs
    Board.none() - the default: no leaderboard, no ranks
    Board.own("finale") results.write your mod's own board, kept apart from the normal ones - read it with api.boards()
    Board.song() results.write the song's normal leaderboard, like a Speaker fight (the server's admin can close it to mods)

    Only runs the server resimulated itself and that were played to the end ever count - a stopped or cancelled fight never does.

    List<LeaderboardEntry> best = api.boards().top("finale", fight.song(), 10);
    api.boards()
    top(board, chart, limit) the best of your board on that chart, best first (up to 100)
    best(board, chart, playerUuid) one player's entry
    names() your boards that have entries

    The result¶

    onResult is called exactly once per fight that started - however it ends: played to the end, given up, stopped by you, cancelled by an admin, a player left, the server stops. It gets a FightResult:

    .onResult(result -> {
    FighterResult me = result.first();
    switch (me.outcome()) {
        case WON -> giveLoot(player);
        case LOST, FORFEITED -> player.sendSystemMessage(Component.literal("The boss laughs."));
        case DRAW, CANCELLED -> { }
    }
})
    FightResult
    reason() why it ended - SONG_END, FORFEIT, PLAYER_OFFLINE, STOPPED_BY_ADDON, PAUSE_TIMEOUT, ADMIN, RESTARTED, SERVER_STOPPING, NOT_STARTED, TIMED_OUT, NOT_SCORED
    winner() the winner's FighterRef, null for a draw or when nobody won
    first(), second() each fighter's FighterResult: outcome, score, accuracy, hits, rank, verified
    cancelled(), isDraw() ended without a winner and not as a draw / played to the end with equal results
    message() the reason the players saw (your stop's text)
    countsForBoard() the results went on a leaderboard

    verified() on a fighter's line says the server resimulated that run itself (the dummy: the server played it) - check it before you hand out anything valuable. A fight cut short (given up, stopped) has the numbers its games reported, verified = false.

    Against a player, one who leaves loses (PLAYER_OFFLINE, the other one wins); against the dummy a player who leaves ends the fight without a winner.

    Get the song ready first¶

    Showing a cutscene before the fight? Let the players' games fetch the song meanwhile, so the fight starts without a loading pause:

    api.fights().prepare(List.of(player), "finale");
// ... your cutscene ...
api.fights().start(request);

    The files a player's game doesn't have yet come in the background (players without MineFunkin are skipped). Needs fights.start.
